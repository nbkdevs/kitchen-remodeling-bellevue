# Privacy policy

Kitchen Remodeling Cost Planner (Android app), the Cabinet & Countertop Measure browser extension, the
Walnut Morning theme and the `kitchen-remodeling-cost` libraries do not collect, store or share personal data.

- All calculations run on your device. Nothing is sent to a server.
- The Android app keeps your last inputs on the device (Android shared preferences) so they are there next time. Uninstalling the app removes them.
- The browser extension keeps your last measurements in the popup's local storage. It requests no browser permissions and makes no network requests.
- No ads, analytics, tracking or accounts.

Opening the website link in the app's About screen opens your browser; that site has its own policy:
https://wakitchenremodelingbellevue.com/privacy/

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/nbkdevs/kitchen-remodeling-bellevue/issues
