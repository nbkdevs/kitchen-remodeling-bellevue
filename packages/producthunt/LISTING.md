**Description:**
Most remodel budgets go wrong in the last 20%: sales tax, the permit and the contingency nobody planned. Enter what you can spend, and Kitchen Remodeling Cost Planner first takes out the permit, the reserve and the tax. It then splits the rest across 12 line items, from cabinets (25%) to plumbing (3%). It can also run the other way: from a construction price to an all-in total. The maths is integer-cent exact, so the lines always add up. It ships as an Android app, a browser calculator, a Docker API, an AI agent skill, and libraries for JavaScript, Python, Rust, Dart, .NET, Ruby and PHP.

**Maker comment:**
We remodel kitchens in Bellevue, WA, and kept explaining the same budget split to homeowners. So we published the maths, MIT licensed. Tax defaults to Bellevue's 10.3%; change it to your city's rate.
