Where the numbers come from

  • Line item shares: HomeGuide's kitchen remodel cost breakdown (cabinets 25%, labor 15%, appliances 15% and so on).
  • Presets: Cost vs Value 2025, Pacific region averages for a 200 sq ft kitchen: minor midrange $29,728, major midrange $86,480, major upscale $171,950.
  • Sales tax: 10.3% is the Bellevue, WA combined rate for October to December 2026. Washington taxes the full contract price, labor included. Change it for your address.
  • Permit allowance is a planning number, not a fee quote.
