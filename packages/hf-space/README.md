# Kitchen Remodeling Budget Calculator

A free kitchen remodeling budget calculator that runs entirely in the browser.

- **Split my budget**: enter your all-in number; sales tax, a contingency reserve and a permit allowance come out first, and the rest is split across 12 line items (cabinets, labor, appliances, countertops, flooring, lighting and more).
- **Price a scope**: enter a construction cost or pick a Cost vs Value 2025 preset (Pacific region: minor midrange $29,728, major midrange $86,480, major upscale $171,950); reserve, tax and permits are added on top.
- Adjust any line item share, the tax rate (default 10.3%, Bellevue WA, Oct to Dec 2026), the reserve and the permit allowance.
- Copy the itemised table as CSV. Integer-cent maths, so the lines always add up to the total.
